How the call is made

1 · Momentum per ETF

0.7 × 1M + 0.3 × (3M − 1M)

The last month carries most of the weight, reflecting current market mood. The two months before it confirm the trend.

2 · Score per phase

Σ (sign × vs market) ÷ Σ |sign|

Signs: ++ = 2, + = 1, − = −1, −− = −2. Each sector is measured against the average of all sectors, so a market-wide rise or fall does not tilt the result. A phase scores high when its usual leaders beat the market and its usual laggards trail it.

Read with care

  • This reads the phase from which sectors lead, not from economic data. A one-off shock, such as an oil spike, can move it.
  • It measures price momentum, which shows where money is leaning. It does not measure fund flows.
  • Fidelity's patterns come from US stocks, 1962–2021. Past patterns may not repeat.
